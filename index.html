Discover interesting facts and care tips about your favorite furry friends.

Dogs (Canis lupus familiaris)

Dogs are known as "man's best friend." They are highly loyal, intelligent, and come in various breeds. They require regular exercise, a good diet, and lots of love.

Cats (Felis catus)

Cats are independent yet affectionate pets. They are known for their agility and playful nature. Perfect for indoor living, they are relatively low-maintenance.

Rabbits

Rabbits are social and curious creatures. They need plenty of space to hop around and a diet rich in hay and fresh vegetables. They can be very friendly if handled gently.

Parrots

Parrots are colorful, intelligent birds capable of mimicking human speech. They need mental stimulation and a lot of social interaction with their owners.
